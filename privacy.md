# Quiet Night OS - Privacy Policy

[English](#english) | [Türkçe](#türkçe)

Last updated: 22 November 2026

## English

Quiet Night OS ("the app") is made by Mustafa Indap ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your
information on iPhone and Apple Watch.

### In short

- We do not collect any of your data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no
  advertising and no tracking. It makes no internet connections of its own.
- Everything the app measures or reads is processed and stored on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing reaches us.

### What the app uses, and where it stays

| Information | What it is used for | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Heart rate and wrist movement from Apple Watch during a night or nap you start | Estimating sleep stages and detecting sleep disruptions; the cues you turned on | On your Apple Watch, then sent to your own iPhone over Apple's watch connection |
| Apple Health data you allow (for example sleep, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, activity and body measurements) | Your Sleep, Recovery and Calm night scores, key metrics and charts | Read on your iPhone; the app's copy is stored on your iPhone and excluded from iCloud and computer backups |
| Microphone, only while "Listen with iPhone tonight" is on | Apple's on-device sound classifier recognises sounds such as screaming, shouting or crying | No audio is recorded or saved. Only the sound type, a confidence value and a relative sound level are kept on your iPhone |
| Your diary, morning answers, naps, wake-up times and settings | Your Journal, reports and the wake-up you choose | On your iPhone (and the watch for the current night) |
| HomeKit lamp (optional, developer option) | Dimming a lamp you choose at the bedside | Controlled through Apple's Home framework on your iPhone |

The app does not write health data to Apple Health. To read heart rate all night, the watch runs the night as a
workout session; that workout is discarded every morning and is not saved to Apple Health, Fitness or your
Activity rings.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never stored in iCloud by the app. If you use
iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, some of the app's own recording files may be part of that
backup; the backup is controlled by you and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

### When information leaves your device

Only when you decide. "Prepare export" in More and the Share buttons on a night's data open the iOS share sheet;
you choose the app or person the file goes to. That recipient's own privacy terms then apply. We never receive
these files unless you send them to us yourself.

Apple provides the operating system, Apple Health, the App Store and TestFlight. When you install or update the
app, Apple may process information under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not
receive personal information from Apple about you, apart from the optional, anonymous crash and usage reports
Apple shares with developers when you turn on "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings.

### Permissions

You can allow or deny each permission (Apple Health, Motion & Fitness, Microphone, Notifications, Alarms, Home),
and change your choice at any time in the iOS Settings app or in the Health app under Sharing > Apps. The app
keeps working with less information; some features then stop.

### Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your devices until you delete it. More > "Delete all data" removes the app's diary, check-ins,
wake-up plans, night recordings and copied Health data from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything it
stored. Data in the Apple Health app is not changed by the app.

### Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If a parent or guardian uses it for a child's sleep, all data still
stays on the family's own devices.

### Not a medical device

Quiet Night OS is a wellness and self-tracking app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts
collecting data, the policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.

### Contact

Questions or requests: **quietnightai@gmail.com**

### Bilgiler cihazınızdan ne zaman çıkar

Yalnızca siz karar verdiğinizde. More bölümündeki "Prepare export" ve bir gecenin verilerindeki Paylaş düğmeleri
iOS paylaşım ekranını açar; dosyanın hangi uygulamaya veya kişiye gideceğini siz seçersiniz. Sonrasında alıcının
kendi gizlilik koşulları geçerlidir. Bu dosyaları siz bize göndermedikçe biz hiçbir zaman almayız.

İşletim sistemini, Apple Sağlık'ı, App Store'u ve TestFlight'ı Apple sağlar. Uygulamayı yüklediğinizde veya
güncellediğinizde Apple bilgileri kendi gizlilik politikasına göre işleyebilir
(https://www.apple.com/tr/legal/privacy/). iOS ayarlarında "Uygulama Geliştiricileriyle Paylaş" seçeneğini
açtığınızda Apple'ın geliştiricilerle paylaştığı isteğe bağlı, anonim çökme ve kullanım raporları dışında,
Apple'dan sizinle ilgili kişisel bilgi almayız.

### İzinler

Her izni (Apple Sağlık, Hareket ve Fitness, Mikrofon, Bildirimler, Alarmlar, Ev) verebilir veya reddedebilir,
seçiminizi istediğiniz zaman iOS Ayarlar uygulamasından ya da Sağlık uygulamasında Paylaşma > Uygulamalar
bölümünden değiştirebilirsiniz. Uygulama daha az bilgiyle çalışmaya devam eder; bazı özellikler o zaman durur.

### Verilerin saklanması ve silinmesi

Verileriniz siz silene kadar cihazlarınızda kalır. More > "Delete all data" uygulamanın günlüğünü, sabah
yanıtlarını, uyandırma planlarını, gece kayıtlarını ve kopyalanmış Sağlık verilerini iPhone'unuzdan siler.
Uygulamayı silmek sakladığı her şeyi kaldırır. Apple Sağlık uygulamasındaki veriler uygulama tarafından
değiştirilmez.

### Çocuklar

Uygulama 13 yaşından küçük çocuklara yönelik değildir. Bir ebeveyn veya vasi uygulamayı bir çocuğun uykusu için
kullanırsa da tüm veriler ailenin kendi cihazlarında kalır.

iyileştirmez veya önlemez.
